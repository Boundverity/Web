About

For organisations bringing AI agents and tools into their operations.

We focus on organisations across industries that want to introduce AI agents and tools into their work, including systems that access internal documents, connect to business applications or take actions on behalf of employees.

Our approach centres on your own workflows, policies and risk priorities, so the advice fits how your organisation actually operates.

Services

Four areas, built around how organisations adopt AI.

Our services are being developed around the following areas.

AI adoption and workflow automation

Supporting organisations across industries to identify suitable use cases, integrate AI agents and other AI tools, and introduce automated workflows tailored to their business needs.

AI security assessments

Examining permissions, data access, connected tools, approval controls and failure handling.

Secure integration

Helping organisations design appropriate access restrictions, human oversight, logging and recovery procedures.

Governance and assurance

Documenting controls, responsibilities and assessment evidence to support applicable regulatory requirements and ongoing review.
